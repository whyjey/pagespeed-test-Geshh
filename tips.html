Where to start if you like basketball

This page contains simple recommendations for those who want to train regularly, without overload, and with gradual progress.

Tips for beginners

To enjoy basketball, you don’t need to complicate your training right away. At first, it is important to master stance, basic dribbling, passing, and close-range shooting. It is useful to practice in short but regular sessions. Repetition builds movement stability and confidence in the game.

It is also important to gradually train yourself to look not only at the ball, but also at the space around you. This helps you see teammates, open areas, and decisions in attack faster.

Basic recommendations

  • Start every training session with a warm-up.
  • Practice with both hands.
  • Do not rush to increase difficulty.
  • Focus on shooting technique, not just accuracy.
  • Drink water and don’t skip recovery.

If you need feedback, you can leave a message on the contacts page .
